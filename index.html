Task 1 · 45 min
🔗 Add Research Links with References

Add research images to your design development page.
Every design should connect to a piece of research.

⏱️ 45 minutes

Find sources · 10 min → Place images · 20 min → Add references · 15 min

Remember

research → design idea → development
Without research links, the examiner cannot see this journey.

🔗 Research links in action · Student work: Loulanky Xia

The viewer can trace each design back to its research.

📚 Harvard referencing

Artist's Last Name, First Initial. (Year). Title of the work [Medium].

  • van Herpen, I. (2019). Hypnosis Collection [Photograph].
  • Chalayan, H. (2007). Mechanical Dress [Fashion design].
  • Kusama, Y. (2016). Infinity Mirrors [Installation].

Put each reference near its image, small (9–10pt).

Steps · click to open

File › Open → your scanned design development page.
On paper? Scan it first at 300 DPI.

Look at each design. Ask:

  • Where did this idea come from?
  • Which research image inspired this shape, texture or detail?
  • Can I show the connection clearly?

Look in your research pages or research folder.

Add each image as a new layer: File › Place Embedded…

Size

Smaller than your designs — they are evidence

Position

Next to the design they inspired

Group

Keep related research and designs together

  • Choose the Type Tool (T)
  • Use a clear font — your handwriting font or a clean sans-serif
  • Size 9–10pt, near the image
  • Check the format against the examples above
  • Can you trace each design back to research?
  • Are all references correct?
  • Is the layout balanced?

Save: File › Export › Export As… → JPG. Take a screenshot.

📤 Upload Task 1 to Padlet ↗

💡 Tips
  • Do not repeat your whole research page — choose the best images
  • Put research near the design — you add arrows and lines in Task 2
  • 2–3 good links are better than many unrelated images
  • A cropped detail can be better than the whole image

Task 2 · 75 min
〰️ Graphic Elements for Visual Flow

Choose ONE technique.
Use it to guide the eye and connect research to designs.

⏱️ 75 minutes

Choose & review tutorial · 15 min → Make graphic elements · 40 min → Apply & refine · 20 min

Before you start
  • Task 1 is finished
  • Your page is open in Photoshop
  • You have a drawing, texture or image to use
  • You know which tutorial you will follow

Choose your tutorial

〰️
Pattern Brushes

Best for: links between elements, guiding the eye.

  • Remove the background
  • Adjust contrast with Levels
  • Edit › Define Pattern
  • Apply along a Pen Tool path
Open tutorial ↗
✂️
Stencils & Brushes

Best for: bold marks, high contrast, textures.

  • Image › Adjustments › Threshold
  • Select and delete white areas
  • Cut stencil shapes into textures
  • Edit › Define Brush Preset
Open tutorial ↗
🔍
Enlarging Details

Best for: emphasis, colour to connect elements.

  • Select with Marquee or Lasso
  • Copy, paste and resize
  • Filter › Noise › Add Noise
  • Colour overlays with blend modes
Open tutorial ↗

Steps · click to open

Open your tutorial. Watch the video. Read the steps before you start.

  • Pattern Brushes: a drawing or collage piece
  • Stencils: an image to change with Threshold
  • Enlarging Details: research images with details to enlarge

Follow the tutorial, e.g.:

  • Define a pattern or brush
  • Make a stencil shape
  • Select and enlarge details
  • Add colour overlays

📸 Take screenshots as you work — you need them for the report.

Connect

Link research to designs

Flow

Guide the eye across the page

Balance

Do not overcrowd — leave space

Purpose

Every element needs a reason

Ask:

  • Do the elements help — or distract?
  • Is the visual flow clear?
  • Can I see research → design?
  • Keep layers: File › Save As… → PSD
  • For Padlet: File › Export › Export As… → JPG

💡 Visual flow tips
  • Less is more — support your designs, do not cover them
  • Use elements to point to key areas
  • Use colour to connect related parts
  • Try several positions before you decide
  • Step back — look at the whole page from a distance
🆘 Stuck? Try this

Make a selection with the Rectangular Marquee Tool (M). Feather must be 0.
My elements cover my designs
Lower the layer Opacity, or make them smaller.
The page looks too busy
Delete some elements. Less is more.
I forgot the tutorial steps
Open your tutorial again — the links are above.
